# Quickstart & validation: Streaming AI narrative

**Feature**: [spec.md](spec.md) | **Plan**: [plan.md](plan.md) | **Date**: 2026-09-24

Setup, environment and root scripts are unchanged from
[feature 001's quickstart](../001-personal-finance-manager/quickstart.md). This feature adds no
environment variable: `LLM_API_KEY`, `LLM_TIMEOUT_MS` and `LLM_BASE_URL` keep their meaning.
`pnpm install` picks up the two new runtime dependencies, `@anthropic-ai/sdk` (api) and
`react-markdown` (web).
