Design system

Light only. Neutral, precise, generous whitespace. Nunito Sans for everything; numbers always tabular and right-aligned in tables. Amounts are always $1,234.56; negatives are -$120.00 (sign and colour, never colour alone).

Pickers — custom, never the browser's

Both controls are our own: a trigger button plus a floating panel, so the chrome, the type and the focus ring match everything else and behave the same on every OS. The panel escapes card clipping, closes on outside click or Escape, and flips above the trigger when there is no room below.

Static reference: the panels below are drawn open so their design is visible. The interaction rules they must implement are in design-system.md § Pickers.
